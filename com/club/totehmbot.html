Why a mirror works

Mechanism 01

A plan tied to a time and a place beats a plan that is only a wish

“I will do X” is an intention. “At 07:00, in the park downstairs, I do X” is an implementation intention — and across 94 independent tests, that second form produced a medium-to-large effect on actually reaching the goal (d = 0.65).

This is exactly what a Totehm stores: every habit carries its time frequency and its spot. TotehmBot doesn't invent the cue — it fires on the one I wrote.

Gollwitzer & Sheeran (2006). Implementation intentions and goal achievement: a meta-analysis of effects and processes. Advances in Experimental Social Psychology, 38, 69–119.
Mechanism 02

The words you use on yourself change what you can do

Across seven experiments with 585 participants, the pronoun people used to talk to themselves under pressure changed their performance, their anxiety recovery, and whether they read what was coming as a challenge rather than a threat.

Self-talk is not decoration. It is a lever with measurable effects — which is why the voice in TotehmBot is engineered, not decorated.

Kross, Bruehlman-Senecal, Park, Burson, Dougherty, Shablack, Bremner, Moser & Ayduk (2014). Self-talk as a regulatory mechanism: how you do it matters. Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 106(2), 304–324.
Mechanism 03

The excuse is the moment. Not the morning.

A repulsion is the thing I do instead — written by me, in my words, before I needed it. When I send an excuse, TotehmBot doesn't argue and doesn't encourage. It hands me back the sentence I wrote on a clearer day.

That is the one moment a generic reminder cannot cover, and the one moment a mirror can.

Built on the repulsion layer of the Totehm — my own text, quoted back. No advice is generated.
What we don't claim

We don't sell “neuro-linguistic programming”. The evidence for NLP as a branded method is weak — the reference systematic review found ten usable studies, four of five randomised trials showing no significant difference, and concluded there is little evidence it improves health outcomes. We'd rather tell you that than borrow a word that sounds like science. The three mechanisms above are real, replicated, and cited. They are what this is built on.
