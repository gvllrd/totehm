For a gallery

  • Paid commissions, not projects. The client pays a 770 € deposit before anything starts. Nobody is asked to work on a maybe.
  • The artist is one of yours. We bring the brief and the budget; you choose the name that fits it.
  • The piece never travels blind. The owner deposits it at your gallery, it is worked on, and it is collected there.
  • One gallery per city, six months of exclusivity, renewed as long as pieces are made.
  • We handle the client, the brief, the material study, the payment and the after-sale. You bring the artist and the address.

For an artist

  • 55 % of the artwork, paid through the gallery, on a piece that already has a buyer.
  • A brief with a story. Every commission comes from one element of the owner's life — a habit, a vision, a story they wrote. You are not decorating a bag, you are painting someone.
  • The material is studied before you touch it: grain, coating, finish, the primer that holds, the pigments that bend. You get the answer, not the problem.
  • Two weeks per piece, from the day the brief is signed.
  • Your name travels with the work, on the piece's certificate.

How a commission runs

  1. An owner deposits a project and pays 770 €. Photo or video of the piece, the exact product, their city, their budget, and the element of their life to paint.
  2. The material is read automatically, then by us. If the piece cannot take the work, the deposit is refunded in full and nothing else happens.
  3. We brief the partner gallery of that city. The gallery proposes an artist for the budget and the style.
  4. The owner deposits the piece at the gallery. The artist paints it there, or at their studio under the gallery's responsibility.
  5. Two weeks later the work is back at the gallery, photographed and certified.
  6. The owner collects it. The artist and the gallery are paid on the artwork, the deposit counts towards it.
The agreement, in one page

We sign one page, by email, and it says exactly this:

· The gallery is the sole intermediary for TOTEHM in its city for six months, renewed tacitly as long as at least one piece is made per period.

· The money. The artwork is invoiced by TOTEHM to the owner. The artist receives 55 % and the gallery 20 % of the artwork, paid within 14 days of the owner collecting the piece. The 770 € deposit is TOTEHM's and counts towards the artwork.

· The piece. It is deposited at the gallery by its owner and stays under the gallery's custody and insurance for the duration of the work, as for any work on consignment. TOTEHM is not the custodian.

· The work. Two weeks from the signed brief. The artist keeps authorship; TOTEHM and the gallery may photograph and show the finished piece.

· Leaving. Either side can end it with 30 days' notice. Commissions already started are finished and paid.

· TOTEHM is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any of the brands whose pieces are painted.
