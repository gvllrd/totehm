1 · We study the piece

You send one photo. We look at the material, its grain, its coating — leather takes the work differently from coated canvas, and some finishes refuse it altogether. That answer comes first, and it costs you nothing: if the piece cannot be painted, the deposit is refunded in full.

2 · We prepare it

This is the step nobody sees and the only one that decides whether the work lasts: degreasing, a light abrasion of the factory finish, hardware and linings masked, then an adhesion primer chosen for that exact material. A paint laid on an unprepared surface looks perfect for a week and cracks at the first fold.

3 · We paint it by hand

Leather-grade acrylics — pigments made to bend with the material, never a hardware-store paint — laid in thin layers, each one dried before the next. Your element becomes the artwork, painted by the artist. Nothing is printed, nothing is a transfer, nothing is a sticker.

4 · We make it hold

The paint is heat-set, then sealed with a flexible top-coat — matte or satin, your call — that takes water, UV and the folds of a piece you actually wear. The piece then rests, we check it under light, and only then does it travel back to you.

The deposit

500 € deposits the project: it pays for the study, the tests on your material and a slot in the workshop. If the piece cannot take the work, it comes back to you. If it can, we tell you the price of the artwork before anything is painted, and the deposit counts towards it.
