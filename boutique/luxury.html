The luxury totehmization is for TotehmPaper owners.

1 · We read the material — 100 % bespoke

Nothing is painted before your piece is studied. Your photo or video is read automatically, then by us: the material, its texture, its grain, its coating. Leather takes the work differently from coated canvas, and some finishes refuse it altogether. The primer and the paint are then chosen for that piece alone. That answer comes first, and it costs you nothing: if the piece cannot be painted, the deposit is refunded in full.

2 · We find your artist, where you are — local artists, always

Ask from Los Angeles, and the artist is from Los Angeles. It is written into the TOTEHM DNA. The mise en relation goes through a partner art gallery — the official and unavoidable intermediary — and we draw on the largest private network of galleries and artists there is. Your city, your budget and the style you chose decide who we go after: the higher the budget, the more prestigious the names we allow ourselves to approach. We give ourselves 30 days; past that, without an artist, you are refunded in full.

3 · We prepare the piece

The step nobody sees, and the only one that decides whether the work lasts: degreasing, a light abrasion of the factory finish, hardware and linings masked, then an adhesion primer mixed for that exact material. A paint laid on an unprepared surface looks perfect for a week and cracks at the first fold.

4 · The artist paints it by hand

Leather-grade pigments — made to bend with the material, never a hardware-store paint — laid in thin layers, each one dried before the next. Your element becomes the artwork. Nothing is printed, nothing is a transfer, nothing is a sticker. The artist has two weeks to finish and to drop the work at the partner gallery.

5 · It holds

The paint is heat-set, then sealed with a flexible top-coat — matte or satin, your call — that takes water, UV and the folds of a piece you actually wear.

The deposit

770 € deposits the project: it pays for the study of your material, the tests on it and the search for your artist. If no artist is secured within 30 days, or if the piece cannot take the work, you are refunded in full. If it can, we tell you the price of the artwork before anything is painted, and the deposit counts towards it.
