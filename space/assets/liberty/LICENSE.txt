Statue of Liberty 3D Photogrammetry Model
==========================================

Source: "Statue Of Liberty Bronze Model" by jerryfisher
URL: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/statue-of-liberty-bronze-model-5b9aa58d51624f01b9009b52379bf18a
Origin: Photogrammetric scan of a 19th-century bronze cast of the Statue of Liberty by Frédéric-Auguste Bartholdi.
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
License URL: http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Modifications:
- Derived and fitted to Historic American Engineering Record measurements (HAER NY-138)
- Re-textured with weathered copper verdigris patina and gilded torch flame
- Optimized into Level-of-Detail GLB files (liberty_statue_l0.glb and liberty_statue_l1.glb)
- Positioned in the submerged ocean of the New York coastal space
