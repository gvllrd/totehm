Photographed weatherworn stone surface. Locally hosted material maps;
there is no request to Poly Haven at runtime. The source contains no joints:
the gallery's individual worn slabs and mortar seams are real geometry.

Original 2048 x 2048 source files:

https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/2k/rock_01/rock_01_nor_gl_2k.jpg
https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/2k/rock_01/rock_01_rough_2k.jpg

ancient-color.webp: source diffuse photograph, neutral warm-grey limestone
grading; original photographed pores, inclusions and scratches retained.
ancient-normal.webp: source OpenGL tangent-space normal map, no colour-space
conversion; local WebP encoding.
ancient-roughness.webp: source measured roughness remapped by 0.12 + 0.78R for
the worn satin stone centres and rough mineral cavities; local WebP encoding.
The matching *-1k.webp files are 1024 x 1024 mip-friendly mobile derivatives.

2k maps are used on desktop; 1k maps on mobile, in linear colour space except
the sRGB diffuse photograph. All share repeat wrapping and anisotropic filtering.
