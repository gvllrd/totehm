sea-normal.webp and sea-foam.webp are original procedural data textures generated
for TOTEHM from seeded periodic Fourier fields and periodic Voronoi cells.
No photographs, external artwork, or third-party texture files are included.
These files follow the terms of the repository; no additional attribution is required.

Normal data: RGB tangent normal, decoded with rgb * 2 - 1; R is world X,
G is world Z, B is the upward normal component. NoColorSpace; flipY=false.
Foam data: R is the irregular cellular foam skin, G is broad patch coverage,
B is the packed microbubble detail. NoColorSpace; flipY=false.
Both images tile exactly. Use RepeatWrapping and trilinear mipmapped filtering.

Encoding: 1024 × 1024 RGB, WebP lossless. Packed channels must not use lossy
WebP/JPEG, whose chroma subsampling changes independent data channels.
Normal channels are quantized to 6 bits before lossless encoding; foam
channels are quantized to 4 bits.
