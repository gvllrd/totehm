How it works

the controller

Move
Push the stick, or swipe anywhere — one finger, two fingers on a trackpad, or drag the radar. Up create · down my space · left by intention · right tomorrow & beyond. Touch the stick: back to live & today.
Zoom
Pinch — fingers or trackpad — or + −. Zoom out far enough and the radar becomes the planet: every Spot on Earth, as lights. Touch a light to fly there. ◎ brings you home.
The ring
Grab the ring around the radar and turn it. Close up, the map turns and the top is where you aim — the Spots ahead are counted. Far out, the planet spins. Two taps on the ring: north. On a phone, align · compass turns the radar with you.

a Spot

A Spot
One of a member's habits, at a date, an hour and a place, offered to a few members.
Places
How many members can come.
Automatic
First come, first in — until every place is taken.
Manual
The creator reads who asks — a name and a compatibility — and chooses.
Public space
A park, a café, a beach, a gym. The radar shows the point within ~100 m; the exact meeting point goes to the members accepted.
Private
Inside the member's own place — a home, a studio, a garage gym. The radar shows only the neighbourhood (~1 km); the address goes only to the members accepted.
FIGHER members
Every member of the club can ask to join.
Subscribers
Only the members who follow this TOTEHM — FIGHER members too.
Social · Silent
Together, and we talk — or together, without talking.
Compatibility
One number: my TOTEHM against this Spot. Never a person against a person.
