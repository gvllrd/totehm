How it works

a Spot

A Spot
One of a member's habits, at a date, an hour and a place, offered to a few members.
Places
How many members can come.
Automatic
First come, first in — until every place is taken.
Manual
The creator reads who asks — a name and a match — and chooses.
Public space
A park, a café, a beach, a gym. The map shows the point within ~100 m; the exact meeting point goes to the members accepted.
Private
Inside the member's own place — a home, a studio, a garage gym. The map shows only the neighbourhood (~1 km); the address goes only to the members accepted.
FIGHER members
Every member of the club can ask to join.
Subscribers
Only the members who follow this TOTEHM — FIGHER members too.
Social · Silent
Together, and we talk — or together, without talking.
Match
The number under a Spot — 62% — is how close this Spot is to my TOTEHM: my intentions, my pillars, my habits and what they are for. One number, never the detail. It measures a Spot against a TOTEHM — never a person against a person.
By …
The name of the creator's TOTEHM. Touch it: it opens that TOTEHM.
I will be here
A Spot to come. I am here: it is happening. I was here: it is done.

a Short-Live

Short-Live
A moment: five seconds filmed where I am, now. A Spot is an experience others join; a moment is only seen.
Film one
Push the stick left, then touch it. REC — five seconds — then which habit of my TOTEHM it is, social or silent, and the GPS shield.
GPS shield
Off: everyone sees the city and the moment, never the point. On: my subscribers see the exact place too. On is not an invitation.
24 hours
A moment stays in the feed one day. I am here the first hour, I was here after.

the site

Radar
What happens today around me — rings, points, no map. Touch a point to read it.
Move
Push the stick, or swipe anywhere — one finger, two fingers on a trackpad. Up create a Spot · down search · left Short-Live · right every Spot to come. Touch the stick: back to the radar.
Search
Touch Habit ▾ to change what you search by: habit, mood or music, mode, date, time, distance.
Meeting point
Only when creating a Spot: place it on the map. The street map fills the screen with a cross in the middle. Move it under the cross, pinch, then Here.
Zoom
Pinch — fingers or trackpad — or + −. Zoom out far enough and the radar becomes the planet: every Spot on Earth, as lights. ◎ brings you home.
The ring
Grab the ring around the radar and turn it. Close up, the top is where you aim. Far out, the planet spins. Two taps on the ring: north.
My space
Top left, next to the white dot — that dot is me. My TOTEHM, my access, who is waiting for my answer.
