01 Fight Higher
Conquer yourself through harder effort. One more rep.
Resilience · Mental hardness · Self-overcoming · Exit the comfort zone · Embrace the pain
Beta / Gamma · Noradrenaline · Endorphins
Habits — Nature & Examples
  • Morning cold shower daily
  • Martial arts training 3×/week
  • Running / trail / obstacles daily or weekly
  • Weight lifting / Crossfit several daily
  • Intermittent fasting daily
  • Ice bath / cryotherapy weekly
  • Waking at 5am+ every morning
  • Doing the task you fear first daily
  • No social media before 9am every morning
  • Writing fears down and facing one weekly
  • Sparring / competitive sports every weekday
  • No phone for 1h after waking every morning
City Spots & Events
  • Boxing gyms, MMA, Muay Thai dojos
  • Crossfit boxes, functional training
  • Open-air workout parks
  • Stadium stairs / urban trail routes
  • Sparring sessions (member drops)
  • 5am run groups
  • Obstacle race events
  • Cold plunge facilities
  • Military-style boot camps
  • Intense dance battles / breaking circles
  • Climbing walls, parkour spots
  • Silent 6am city runs (member coord.)
Habit Classification Signals
  • Keywords: discipline, pain, harder, push, resist, cold, early, training, conquer, one more
  • Physical effort markers: weights, run, spar, fast, wake
  • Frequency: daily/morning-heavy habits → Fight
  • Avoidance patterns (no X before Y) → Fight
  • Self-competition language ("beat my record") → Fight
  • Habit requiring willpower against impulse → Fight
Default Mentor
David Goggins
Can't Hurt Me · Stay Hard · 40% Rule
Mentor Tone Signature
  • Zero comfort. Zero excuses.
  • Military cadence — short, punchy sentences
  • Orders, not suggestions
  • Uses "you're soft" energy to trigger action
  • Celebrates completion, never the feeling
  • Quantifies everything (reps, time, weight)
Push Message Tone — Bot Examples
Your body is lying to you right now. It's telling you to stay warm. Don't. The cold shower is waiting. 30 seconds. Go.
5:47am. You're already behind every person who got up before you. That's data. Use it. Shoes on.
That session you're about to skip? That's exactly the session you need. The discomfort IS the point.
