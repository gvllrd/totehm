Our simple terms

totehm.com & TotehmBot · last updated 28 July 2026

No lawyer wrote this to protect us from you. It says what we do with what you give us, in words you can actually check.

What TOTEHM is

TOTEHM is a tool for building habits around seven intentions. It runs at totehm.com and, if you choose to connect it, through TotehmBot on Telegram. It is not a medical service, and nothing it tells you is medical advice.

Your Totehm belongs to you

The habits you write, the objectives you set and the chapters your Totehm writes are yours. We do not sell them, rent them, or hand them to advertisers.

What we store

  • Your habits, objectives and intentions — so your Totehm exists between sessions.
  • Your events — each addition, removal and change, kept as an append-only trail. This is the raw material your Book is written from.
  • Your email — used to sign you in. We use one-time codes; there is no password to lose.
  • Your city — only if you allow location, and only the city name. We do not keep your coordinates.
  • Your Telegram id — only if you connect TotehmBot, so it knows which Totehm it is speaking to.
  • Your conversations with TotehmBot — so it remembers what you told it.

What we send to others

Two services see fragments of your data so the product can work:

  • OpenAI — receives the objective you type, to generate and grade habits. It does not receive your identity.
  • OpenStreetMap — receives your coordinates once, to turn them into a city name.

Nothing else leaves. No analytics resold, no tracking pixels, no data brokers.

Generated habits are suggestions

Habits are generated by a language model and graded Essential, Recommended or Experimental according to how strong the evidence is. That grading is produced by a model, not by a clinician, and the model has no live access to the literature.

Read the Why report before you trust a habit, and never treat it as a substitute for a doctor.

Subscriptions

  • Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel.
  • You can cancel at any time. Access stays open until the end of the period you already paid for.
  • We do not charge for the following period once you have cancelled.
  • Prices may change. If they do, you will be told before the change applies to you, and you are free to leave.
  • We never see your card. Payment is handled by our payment provider.

Deleting your Totehm

You can wipe your Totehm from the settings: habits, objectives, events and Book chapters are permanently deleted. This cannot be undone and we keep no copy.

Deleting your Totehm does not cancel your subscription. Those are two separate actions, on purpose — so wiping the slate never costs you the access you paid for. Cancel from your subscription settings if that is what you want.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of everything we hold on you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to erase all of it including your account. Write to hello@higher.boutique and we answer within 30 days.

What we ask of you

  • Be at least 16, or have a parent's permission.
  • One account per person. Do not resell access.
  • Do not use TotehmBot to harm anyone, yourself included.

If something goes wrong

TOTEHM is provided as it is. We keep it running as best we can, but we cannot promise it will never break. We are not liable for what you decide to do with the habits it suggests — those decisions stay yours.

When these terms change

If we change something that matters, we will say so in the app before it takes effect. Continuing to use TOTEHM after that means you accept the new version.
