04 · Questions

The obvious stuff.

Why a complete TOTEHM?

Because the TOTEHM is the passport. The club is built around people who already gave their life a structure across the five views. Complete means one written box in each view.

Why the TotehmPaper?

It is the physical rule of the club: a tangible object for working with your strategy. You get it on higher.boutique.

Do I need an audience to monetize?

No. Every member can open a subscription to their TOTEHM. A few subscribers are enough: small amounts accumulate in your balance and are paid out together.

Does a subscriber see my TOTEHM?

Only if you include it in your benefits. And it goes one way: a subscriber reads yours, you do not read theirs.

Does applying to a Spot open my TOTEHM?

No. The creator sees your name and one compatibility number with that Spot. Never your boxes, never a breakdown.

Can I leave?

Yes, from your console. Your membership runs until the end of the period you paid for.
